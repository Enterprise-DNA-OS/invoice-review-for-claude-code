# Target research, 2026-10-31

Rossum was selected for the new invoice-exception-management family. Purchasing creates orders; supplier-statement reconciliation compares ledgers; this desk checks captured invoices before accounting handoff.

Rossum's [pricing page](https://rossum.ai/pricing/) lists Starter from $18,000 per year without naming the currency in the visible price. This is a public starting price, not a confirmed ANZ customer's annual invoice. Higher plans are quote-based. Public [export documentation](https://knowledge-base.rossum.ai/help/docs/exporting-captured-data) documents CSV and repeating headers for line items. Search results contain [G2 alternatives](https://www.g2.com/products/rossum/competitors/alternatives) and [Capterra alternatives](https://www.capterra.com/p/193772/Rossum/alternatives/); search volume was not measured and review complaints are not used as product claims.

Salesforce's [Public Sector pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/government/solutions/pricing/) lists a broader platform at USD 275 per user per month billed annually. Twenty seats imply USD 66,000 a year, not an inspection module quote or observed customer bill. It overlaps an existing licensing build. [OneTrust pricing](https://www.onetrust.com/pricing/) provides no current public annual price for the candidate and overlaps privacy operations. Neither was registered.

The selected scope is supervised captured-data review. Rossum's wider extraction, mailbox, archive and integrations are not claimed as features of the free base. No competitor exclusivity is claimed for the ten included questions; equivalent custom reports are possible elsewhere.
