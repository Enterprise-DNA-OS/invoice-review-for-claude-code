# Weekly review

Run `node scripts/invoice.mjs attention --json`, `node scripts/invoice.mjs orders --json` and `node scripts/invoice.mjs handoff-ready --json`.

Write a dated Monday review to drafts/ from these three current reads. Name overdue invoices, receipt gaps and ready handoffs, with owner and evidence reference. Keep amounts in their recorded currencies. Summarise decisions still needed. Never send, post or pay.
